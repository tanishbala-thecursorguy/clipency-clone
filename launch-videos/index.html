Questions

Launch video FAQ.

What is a startup launch video?

A short film that introduces a new company or product to its market. Clipency makes launch videos as a series of 5–6 films covering the team, customer, niche, company, problem to solution and product, so buyers get the whole story, not a single ad.

How long does a launch video series take?

The startup launch series rolls out over 1–2 months. A single product launch film for an established company is faster. Distribution begins as soon as the first film is ready.

Do you only make the video, or also get it seen?

Both, if you want. Clipency can make the launch films only, or make them and distribute them through clipping and UGC. Most clients choose the full launch, because a video without reach fades in weeks.

Which companies do you make launch videos for?

Tech products: SaaS, AI tools, consumer apps, online services and platforms, from early-stage startups to established companies launching a new product.

Which platforms are the launch videos made for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts first, with longer cuts for your site, YouTube and product pages.
