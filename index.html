The Rule of Seven

See it once, scroll past.
See it seven times, want it.

Every Clipency launch is built on one marketing rule: frequency builds trust.

Nobody buys from a single ad. People buy what they keep running into: from the founder’s story, then a theme page, then a creator who actually uses it. By the seventh time, it doesn’t feel like marketing. It feels like everyone is talking about you.

So we don’t make one viral video and hope. We engineer the seven.

    Questions

    Before you book.

    Can I buy just one service?

    Yes. Some teams only want launch films, others only want distribution for content they already have, or UGC on its own. Pick what you need. The full engine is there if you want it, never forced.

    What kind of companies do you work with?

    Mostly tech: SaaS, AI tools, consumer apps, online services and platforms. Startups get the full launch series. Established companies skip straight to the product launch and scale.

    How long does a launch take?

    The startup launch series rolls out over 1–2 months. Distribution starts as soon as the first film is ready, and UGC runs month to month for as long as you want the trust to keep compounding.

    Where will my product show up?

    Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts: on theme pages in your niche, on clipping accounts, and on creator pages dedicated to your product.

    Do you only work with big companies?

    No. We work with elite companies and with early-stage startups, and both get the same team and the same respect. If you have a product worth launching, we want to hear about it.

    How do I know it’s working?

    You get a live dashboard with every post, its link, views and the creator behind it. Nothing is hidden behind a monthly report.
