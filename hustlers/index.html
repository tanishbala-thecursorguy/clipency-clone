Questions

Hustler FAQ.

Do I need a big following?

No. New pages can join and start posting from day one. Some UGC campaigns look for a particular niche or audience, and the brief says so up front.

What’s the difference between a clipper and a UGC creator?

Clippers cut and repost existing content, like launch films and long-form videos. UGC creators appear on camera with the product on a page dedicated to it. Clippers see clipping campaigns, creators see UGC campaigns, and you can switch from your profile.

How do I get my Instagram connected?

UGC creators log in with Instagram’s official login, and we never see your password. If you’re only doing a one-time gig, you can verify with a short code in your bio instead.

What if my clip gets rejected?

Every rejection comes with a written reason. If you think it’s wrong, open an appeal and talk it through with the brand right inside your dashboard.

Which platforms can I post on?

Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
